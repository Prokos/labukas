# Chapter 5 teaching review — 27 September 2026

Status: source review and teaching contract in progress. This is an internal review; chapter 5 is not quality-approved.

## Findings before revision

- The existing 79-lesson draft puts the ticket conversation at lesson 10, before its one-/two-ticket replies at lesson 23. The competing reply “from Kaunas” is not prepared in that conversation's teaching.
- Early hobby examples change the verb, activity and grammatical frame together. They do not isolate liking versus ability, or the different noun/person forms after patinka and mėgstu.
- The first past lesson combines two people, two places and two verbs. The first future lesson similarly mixes being, travelling and walking. The useful same-person past/present/future contrast appears around lesson 62.
- Time words are formally introduced near the end, after tense exercises depend on them.
- The source's dative person forms, listening exception, two past stem classes, future person endings and singular/plural instrumental need explicit contrast and application. Inventory presence alone does not demonstrate these functions.
- The reading/writing and travel exchanges need an audit of all assumed words and forms, including departure/arrival, seat/row, return tickets and polite wishes.

## Teaching contract

Use the same activity to contrast liking with ability. Reuse one noun to compare patinka + nominative with mėgstu + accusative, and affirmative with negative objects. Separate a dative experiencer from a changing subject/verb. Introduce yesterday/today/tomorrow before contrasting tense, then isolate person and stem changes with the same verb and place.

Prepare every ticket-conversation option, including quantity and destination/origin contrasts, before asking the learner to transact. Build a useful exchange before wider leisure/travel lists. Extend into companions, reported trips, future arrangements, seat/row and travel reading. A future greeting or phrase introduced as a chunk does not establish tense mastery.

For each function, record exact models, bounded production and later changed-context checks. New combinations must keep target identities and must not duplicate a model or an earlier check. Repeated retrieval remains separate from transfer. Keep immutable history and old unfinished queues; retire truly replaced teaching, not source coverage. Writing remains self-reviewed.
