# Teaching design

A chapter contains focused classes; a lesson is a short visit to one class. Keep the exercise header to one line: Chapter N - Lesson X of Y. Class titles and phases belong to the start and completion screens. The [coverage ledger](coverage.md) inventories the authored material and the [curriculum audit](curriculum-audit.md) records the latest structural review.

## Course teaching

Introduce at most three targets, practise with support, retrieve later, and use the material in context. Reading keeps its passage available and progresses to short typed answers. Writing saves drafts, shows a model after submission, and requires explicit self-review.

Discovery preserves the authored introduction order. Guided rounds vary order; recall is shuffled. Planned stages still require the learner to reach their target level. An easier repair cannot substitute for a harder required task. Checkpoints rotate candidates and require 80% first-attempt accuracy.

General notes appear at the first class introduction and remain available through hints. Feedback shows the expected answer and any authored item explanation; it does not present unrelated notes as a diagnosis.

## Practice and returns

Personal review chooses up to eight earlier targets. Named practice sets reuse the course's small introduction groups; selecting an individual word anchors the set rather than selecting an entire skill category. New targets receive supported rounds. The set is fixed at launch and finishes after its tasks and bounded retries. Repeating practice is an explicit action and does not award a course pass.

Recovered mistakes lose their lifetime error-rate advantage after two successful answers. Recent successful items receive a ten-minute cooldown for incidental course inserts. Due and unresolved items remain priorities. Guided rounds avoid adjacent duplicates when another target at the same stage is available. A one-target correction may still need a repetition.

Support level and memory are separate. Memory intervals advance only when qualifying successes are at least four hours apart. Errors become due in one minute; later intervals are four hours, one day, three days, seven days, fourteen days, and thirty days. These are heuristics, not validated retention measurements.

## Compatibility

Saved events are immutable and merge by UUID. Stable item IDs keep their meaning. Content can declare discovery revisions and teaching-order constraints. A general revision mechanism retains historical groups, carries their completed targets forward, and skips those targets within partially covered groups without awarding uncompleted targets. Previously completed chapter checks keep their original membership. Existing class-completion compatibility remains in place.

## Limits

The answer checker is not a general grammar parser. Explicit alternatives and limited supported pronoun omission cover some variants. The introductory identification sentences explicitly accept versions with and without `yra`; see the [University of Texas Lithuanian grammar notes](https://lrc.la.utexas.edu/eieol/litol/40) for present-tense omission. Diacritics still matter. Writing is self-reviewed and does not infer vocabulary mastery. Review records belong to targets, not complete grammatical concepts. Further work should be guided by actual mistakes, pacing, and delayed recall.
