## Scope

The main path now teaches the chapter vocabulary inventories, explicit grammar families and communicative objectives represented below, with original English guidance and original text activities. The expanded country/language appendix is optional practice, available in Your course without blocking progression. The alphabet and grammar tables are available as a collapsed study reference.

This is a text adaptation: audio, pronunciation assessment, song performance, partner speaking and licensed imagery are excluded. Listening-information objectives receive text-based counterparts. Open writing uses a saved draft, model and checklist; it is self-reviewed, not automatically grammar-graded. The ledger establishes authored coverage, not validated A1 proficiency or a word-for-word reproduction of every printed task or incidental glossary entry.

## Progress and assessment

Original word and ordinary lesson IDs are retained. New application visits for the 23 previously context-free classes have new step IDs, so an old application pass does not award the new work. Historical chapter-check events remain readable; expanded checks use new IDs. Each check section samples two targets per class from at most four classes. Retakes prioritise less-tested targets and weaker support levels rather than always asking the same first/last pair. All sections and teaching visits must be completed to finish the main path. Self-reviewed writing never contributes to automatic word mastery.

## Remaining validation

Native-speaker review and actual delayed-retention observation remain necessary. The app still uses item-level learning records rather than a complete model of morphological knowledge. This content pass does not claim that every accepted Lithuanian paraphrase is covered or that every textbook glossary surface form is independently drilled.
