# Learning contract

Correct answers, spelling corrections, incorrect answers and unassessed open responses are distinct outcomes. Capitalization and ordinary punctuation do not change the answer. Diacritic differences can be spelling errors; known competing Lithuanian forms remain incorrect. Closed vocabulary recall also allows one bounded internal typo in a word of at least five letters, with the first and last two letters preserved. Ambiguous matches and ending changes are not automatically accepted. This bounded rule is not a general grammar analyser.

A spelling correction does not earn exact-form recall. A diacritic-only correction can retain word-identity evidence; a bounded vocabulary typo does not imply independent recall. Open writing is self-reviewed and does not earn automatic word or grammar mastery.

Help has one entry point. A meaningful cue, authored choices and answer reveal are assistance. Merely opening an empty Help menu is not. A first failure receives an unrevealed supported retry; a further failure can show the answer for reconstruction. Repairs and revealed answers never stand in for unaided recall. Matching does not print translations as extra clues and permits selection from either column.

The runtime tracks visible answers and recent exposure, including answer options, models and feedback. Exposure follows the learner into another lesson or Practice. Incidental exposure does not erase established recall, but a primed answer cannot establish a new independent retrieval.

Evidence distinguishes recognition, construction, word recall, form recall and self-reviewed writing. Same-day repetitions do not establish durable retention. “Remembered later” requires unaided recall on separate local dates with at least four hours between qualifying visits. Construction-only targets are assessed as construction rather than forced into typing.

Practice prioritizes unresolved difficulty and due retrieval, uses encountered material, and excludes recently answered easy items when weaker targets are available. Up to two unresolved targets can return after at least two other responses in the next lesson. Practice is bounded by total turns, per-target turns and repeated failures. A tiny target pool ends without claiming spaced recall. Needing further support remains visible after the session ends.

These are conservative evidence rules, not a measured retention guarantee. Planned returns create opportunities; only the learner's recorded unaided responses establish recall. Validate teaching through later-day retrieval and unfamiliar combinations, not lesson completion alone.
