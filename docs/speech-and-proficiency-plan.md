# Speech and proficiency boundaries

## Current implementation

Deliver the curriculum through text, following the [product plan](product-learning-plan.md). Listening and speaking are deferred. No recording UI, speech backend, paid calls or provider benchmark is needed for this release.

While authoring, retain useful future listening/speaking opportunities as optional metadata: stable goal/utterance reference, modality, availability and a real text fallback where applicable. Planned variants are invisible, never required and earn no completion. Every unit remains completable without audio; text attempts earn text evidence.

## Proficiency

The eventual target is A2, with an A1 milestone. The 36-unit outline is neither a content ceiling nor a proficiency guarantee. The opening six units do not establish A1. A text-only release cannot establish listening/speaking proficiency or all-skills A2.

At A2, intended abilities include understanding essential information in short everyday messages and speech; finding information in notices, menus and timetables; exchanging routine information; describing everyday life; and writing short practical notes. Use the [official CEFR descriptors](https://europass.europa.eu/en/common-european-framework-reference-language-skills) to map goals and assessments by skill.

Connect each claimed ability to teaching, supported practice, independent practice and reserved checks. Test delayed recall and new contexts. Calibrate exit tasks with assessment expertise before advertising a CEFR outcome. Put these boundaries in course information and accurate skill labels, rather than disclaimers after every lesson. B1 is a later expansion.

## When speech work is scheduled

Start listening with Google Chirp recordings reviewed by a Lithuanian speaker before publication. Store approved clips/transcripts as versioned reusable assets. Preserve a text route and “Can’t listen now” option; showing a transcript does not count as unaided listening.

Speaking needs separate validation. Transcription can support feedback on words and meaning, but cannot establish pronunciation quality. Recording/repeating a model is practice, not demonstrated independent speaking. Native-speaker judgments are needed to validate spoken outcomes.

The [deferred research reference](reference/speech-research.md) contains provider candidates, production requirements, speaking activities and dated cost scenarios. Consult it when speech work starts.
